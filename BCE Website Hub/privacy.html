Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Bala Construction & Engineering Pte Ltd (“BCE”, “we”, “us”) is a company registered in Singapore under UEN 202413369H. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It is written to meet our obligations under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

By using this website or submitting an enquiry, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not submit your details through the site — you are welcome to call us instead.

1. What personal data we collect

When you complete an enquiry form on this website, we collect the details you enter:

  • Your full name
  • Your contact number
  • Your email address
  • The address and postal code of the property the work relates to
  • The building type and the service or services you are interested in
  • Any description of the issue you choose to give us

If you contact us by phone, email or messaging app instead, we collect whatever details you choose to share in that conversation.

We do not ask for and do not want your NRIC number, financial details, or any other sensitive information through this website. Please do not send those to us through the enquiry form.

2. Optional features that use your device

Two parts of the enquiry form are optional and only run if you choose to use them:

Use my current location

If you tap the location icon in the address field, your browser will ask for permission to share your location. If you allow it, your approximate coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to convert them into a street address, which is then filled into the form for you. We do not store the raw coordinates. If you decline the permission, nothing is sent and you can simply type your address instead.

Voice input

The message field offers a voice input button. This uses your browser’s own speech recognition. Depending on your browser, your speech may be sent to the browser maker’s servers to be transcribed. That processing is governed by your browser’s privacy policy, not ours. You can always type instead.

3. Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider records standard technical information when a page is requested, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. This is used to keep the site running, secure and performing properly.

This site loads its typeface from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your IP address is visible to Google as part of that request. See Google’s Privacy Policy for how they handle it.

4. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site uses a small amount of storage inside your own browser for the following, and none of it is sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Remembering that you have already seen the opening animation, so it does not replay on every page
  • Holding a copy of an enquiry you have submitted, so a confirmation can be shown to you

This data stays on your device. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

5. Why we use your personal data

We use the details you give us to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and answer your questions
  • Prepare and send you a quotation
  • Arrange a site visit, schedule works and carry out the service you asked for
  • Contact you about work in progress, including follow-up and defects
  • Keep proper business and accounting records

We will not use your details to send you marketing messages unless you have separately agreed to receive them. Where we send marketing messages to Singapore telephone numbers, we check the Do Not Call (DNC) Registry first, as required by the PDPA.

6. Consent and withdrawing it

We collect your personal data with your consent, which you give by submitting the form or by contacting us directly. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us using the contact details below.

If you withdraw consent we will stop using your data for the purposes above. Please note this may mean we cannot continue to provide a service or complete work already in progress. We may still need to keep certain records to meet legal and accounting obligations.

7. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else’s marketing. We share it only where it is necessary:

  • Our form delivery service. Enquiries submitted on this site are passed through Web3Forms, which delivers them to our email inbox. They process the submission on our behalf.
  • Our email and hosting providers, who store and transmit the message for us.
  • Sub-contractors and suppliers, but only the details they need to carry out your job — typically the site address and a contact number.
  • Authorities or professional advisers, where we are required to by law.

Some of these providers operate servers outside Singapore. Where personal data is transferred overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA.

8. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as there is a business or legal reason to do so. In practice that means:

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work — up to 12 months, in case you come back to us
  • Records relating to completed work — for as long as required for warranty, defects and accounting purposes

After that, we delete the records or remove anything that identifies you.

9. Keeping your data safe

This website is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection, so what you type into the form is protected in transit. Access to our enquiry inbox is limited to the people who need it. We review these measures from time to time.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach occurs that is likely to cause significant harm, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commission and affected individuals as the PDPA requires.

10. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and tell you how it has been used in the past year
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate or out of date
  • Withdraw your consent to our continued use of it

Write to our Data Protection Officer using the details below. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can, and within the timeframes set by the PDPA. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. A reasonable fee may apply to access requests, and we will tell you in advance if so.
